# 📱 VOTESMART APPLICATION
The application to help filipino make smarter voting decisions

**🪛 Features**
- Candidate Background Check 
- Precinct & Voting Information 
- Voter Eligibility Checker 
- Notifications & reminders 
- Security & Privacy 

** Goals ** 
- 📖Program aims to address lack of voter education in the Philippines

- 📢 Our Porgam aims to address awareness in what the candidates are not showing the public so that they understand more of the personality and determine wether or not the candidate is fit.

- 📊 Most importantly our program aims to reform the government heirarchy that has lead the Filipinos astray
